abstract: >-
  GeoOpen3D adapts visual foundation models to outdoor point-cloud segmentation
  without training a 3D network. Geometry-preserving RGB-D projections improve
  the input to GroundingDINO and SAM, while depth-aware constraints select
  coherent masks. Pixel-to-point mappings then transfer the resulting labels
  into 3D. Evaluations on urban and island scenes examine segmentation quality
  and transfer to categories outside a fixed label set.
