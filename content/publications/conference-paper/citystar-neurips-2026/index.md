abstract: >-
  CitySTAR treats language-guided localization in city-scale point clouds as
  structured constraint reasoning. Its training-free pipeline builds an
  open-vocabulary scene graph, gathers multimodal evidence with language-model
  tools, and checks target-context topology to distinguish candidate objects.
  A reflective grounding stage combines spatial consistency with visual evidence.
  The accompanying CitySTAR-3D benchmark strengthens instance annotations and
  evaluation of complex spatial relations in urban scenes.
