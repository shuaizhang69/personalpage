abstract: >-
  Holo360D provides continuous panoramic trajectories for studying feed-forward
  3D reconstruction. The dataset contains 109,495 panoramas paired with point
  clouds, meshes and camera poses. Scanner and panoramic-camera measurements
  are aligned through SLAM, followed by geometry cleaning and mesh refinement.
  Reconstruction experiments investigate how fine-tuning on these sequences
  helps models handle panoramic imagery and its spherical distortions.
